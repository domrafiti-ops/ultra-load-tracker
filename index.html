How to read these

A strong positive correlation for Ê vs soreness means the eccentric channel is tracking your actual tissue response. A weak one points at km or your descent step length, not at the descent metres — those are measured, the other two are assumed. Adjust one at a time below and watch the correlation move; because nothing derived is stored, the entire history re-prices instantly.

AeL vs RPE should be strong for continuous sessions. If it is weak specifically on interval days, that is expected and quantified: average heart rate under-counts a session with high intensity spread by roughly 1.843·σ²F, about 7 % at σF = 0.20. Log intervals and the rest of the run as two entries.

What the Access Key does and does not protect

This is a static site, so the key you paste lives in your browser's local storage and is visible to anyone with your browser or devtools. That is why it must be an Access Key scoped to Bins Read + Bins Update — never your Master Key, which can create and delete every bin on your account. Someone who extracted this key could read or overwrite this one training log. For a personal training log that is an acceptable trade; do not put anything else in the bin.
