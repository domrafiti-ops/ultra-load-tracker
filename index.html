Baseline is the three weeks before this one, not the last 28 days — the usual acute:chronic ratio puts the current week inside its own denominator, and that coupling is the heart of the published criticism of the metric. No safe zone is shown, deliberately: the threshold bands you have seen elsewhere have largely failed to replicate, so a number is honest and a verdict would not be.

Descent over 28 days is context for Ê, not part of it. A damaging descent confers weeks of protection against the next one, so the same 1000 m means less to tissue in week 8 of a mountain block than in week 1. That effect is real and well replicated, but calibrating it to you would need data this tool does not have — so it is shown beside Ê for you to read, rather than folded in as a coefficient invented for the purpose.
